# Agent Handoff: What Help Is Needed

This user does not need generic coding help or low-yield knob tuning. They need a high-reliability experiment partner who can find edge, preserve evidence, and reduce wasted spend.

## What The User Is Trying To Do

- Use this competition work to build a real path forward financially and technically.
- Find a real performance edge, not just make tidy incremental changes.
- Turn strong model ideas into legal, reproducible submissions under the byte cap.
- Work from evidence, not vibes, while still being willing to swing big.

## What Has Already Happened

- Big structural changes created the strongest signal.
- Small global knob sweeps mostly did not move the needle enough.
- The strongest over-cap whale signal was `whale_dim576` at `1.07258990`, but illegal on size.
- The best legal whale branch seen was `calib256` at `1.09552035`.
- The key finding is that uniform quant policy is too blunt.
- `mclip11p5` improved score a lot but broke bytes, which strongly suggests the next path is non-uniform quant.

## What Help The User Actually Needs

- A partner who protects experiment quality.
- A partner who saves logs, results, and x-rays rigorously.
- A partner who verifies pod state before making claims.
- A partner who avoids fake confidence and avoids “safe-looking” but low-value sweeps.
- A partner who can translate x-ray evidence into specific ablations.
- A partner who can propose bold tests without losing scientific discipline.

## What To Focus On

1. Freeze the strongest legal donor and treat it like a lab specimen.
2. Stop global scalar quant sweeps unless the evidence is overwhelming.
3. Build layer-aware and tensor-group-aware quant policies.
4. Use x-rays to identify where quantization destroys the gains from extra depth/width.
5. Preserve a clean ledger for every run:
   - raw gain
   - quant drop
   - sliding recovery
   - TTT recovery
   - bytes
6. Kill weak runs early when they already fail the question they were meant to answer.

## What Not To Do

- Do not waste pod time on tiny generic knob festivals.
- Do not treat “over cap” as the only signal during ablation planning.
- Do not claim runs are active without verifying the pod.
- Do not let results live only in chat memory; write them down locally.
- Do not mix multiple unclear variables into one test if the goal is diagnosis.

## Best Current Direction

- The most credible next lane is non-uniform quant:
  - layer-band policies
  - tensor-group policies
  - special treatment for late layers and control tensors
- Structural bets can still matter, but they should be paired with a compression plan from the start.
- Triton / systems work should stay separate as a throughput moonshot, not be mixed into core science unless it proves real speed gains.

## Style The User Responds Best To

- Short factual updates
- Honest uncertainty
- Strong opinions backed by evidence
- Real-time verification
- Big-swing thinking with disciplined execution

## Bottom Line

The user needs an agent who can help them find a real edge without burning time and money on low-signal work. The job is not to look busy. The job is to preserve evidence, sharpen the search, and turn strong model signal into a legal winning path.
